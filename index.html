通道补全之后，跨数据集的“长得像”的药和基因，哪些值得做实验？After channel completion: which cross-dataset look-alike drugs and genes are worth wet-lab tests?

我们有多套显微镜细胞图像数据。有的主要是加药，有的还包含敲掉某个基因或过表达某个基因。 因为荧光通道不齐，以前很难公平比较。把缺失通道补全（我们叫它「统一空间」）之后， 可以在同一套形态特征里问：这个药在形态上最像谁？ 如果它突然很像另一个数据集里的药或基因扰动，就画出一条「邻居边」。 本页告诉实验同事：这些边里，哪些像已知故事、哪些可能是新发现、具体怎么验证、怎样算做成了。 We have multiple microscopy cell-image datasets. Some are mainly drug treatments; others also include gene knockout or overexpression. Uneven fluorescence channels used to make fair comparison hard. After completing missing channels (the “unified space”), we can ask in one morphology feature space: who does this drug look most like? If it suddenly resembles a drug or genetic perturbation from another dataset, we draw a “neighbor edge”. This page tells experimental colleagues which edges look like known stories, which may be new findings, how to validate them, and what counts as success.

建议怎么分步做实验Suggested experimental layers

三层漏斗：先统一复现细胞染色成像，确认形态邻居站得住；再在细胞系上用遗传与组学手段证明「不只是长得像」；最后只挑 1–2 个顶尖发现上活体。Three-layer funnel: first uniformly redo Cell Painting to confirm morphological neighbors; then cell-line genetics/omics to show links beyond look-alike; finally only 1–2 top hits go in vivo.

文献里说的“方向一致 / 方向相反”只是阅读论文后的判断，Literature “same / opposite direction” labels are reading judgments, 不是实验实锤not wet-lab facts。 弱证据边也建议进第一层成像复现，用来统计“形态上像、但复现不像”的比例。 Weak edges should still enter Layer-1 imaging, to estimate how often look-alikes fail to reproduce.

第一层：全员统一复现细胞染色成像（形态邻居复核）Layer 1: Uniform Cell Painting redo (morphology neighbor check)

要验证什么What to test

在同一套细胞染色成像条件下，补全后找出的跨库邻居，是否真的和中心药物“长得像”。这一层主要核对：形态邻居结论能不能在湿实验里站稳。Under one Cell Painting protocol, do Intact cross-library neighbors truly look like the query drug? This layer checks whether morphology-neighbor claims hold in wet lab.

打算怎么做How to do it

尽量覆盖全部候选边。用与药物库一致的乳腺癌细胞系（如 MCF-7）。每个化合物先测活力曲线，选大约只明显影响 10%–30% 细胞的剂量，避免大片死亡造成假象。处理约 24 小时后做多通道细胞染色成像，与溶剂对照、一个已知阳性药、一个预期无关药同板比较。Cover as many candidate edges as practical. Use a breast cancer line matching the drug library (e.g. MCF-7). Dose–response first; pick ~IC10–IC30 to avoid death artifacts. After ~24 h, multi-channel Cell Painting vs DMSO, one known positive, and one expected unrelated drug on the same plate.

怎样算成功/失败Success / failure

形态上仍靠近中心药的边进入第二层；多数弱证据边应偏阴性（阴性同样有用，用来写假阳性率）。意外阳性的弱边升为优先深入。大约 3–6 周，材料费约几万元人民币量级。Edges still morphologically near the query advance to Layer 2; most weak edges should trend negative (negatives still useful for false-positive rate). Unexpected weak positives promote to priority. ~3–6 weeks; materials on the order of tens of thousands RMB.

第二层：细胞系上的分子 / 遗传验证（突出“不只是形态”）Layer 2: Cell-line molecular / genetic validation (beyond morphology)

要验证什么What to test

这些表型邻居是否在基因组、转录组、蛋白组层面也有联系——而不只是显微镜下长得像。重点裁决：真表型模仿、同一大类压力、互相拮抗，还是方向说反了。Whether phenotype neighbors also link at genome / transcriptome / proteome levels — not just look-alike under the microscope. Decide: true phenocopy, shared stress basin, antagonism, or wrong direction.

打算怎么做How to do it

仍在细胞系完成。按各边条文做蛋白质印迹、免疫荧光、细胞周期流式、共处理或挽救；用小干扰 RNA / 过表达等遗传扰动钉通路。有条件时补转录组或蛋白组，把“组学上能说通”写进故事。稳定基因编辑敲除可作为本层加强项，但不必当成最终台阶。Still in cell lines. Follow each edge’s Western / IF / cycle flow / co-treatment or rescue; pin pathways with siRNA / OE. Add transcriptomics or proteomics when available so the story is omics-coherent. Stable CRISPR KO can strengthen this layer but is not the final step.

怎样算成功/失败Success / failure

1 到若干条边能讲清「形态 + 分子/遗传」小故事即可；做出来不像也请保留——说明形态邻居不等于同一机制。大约再加 2–4 个月。One to a few edges with a clear morphology + molecular/genetic story is enough; keep negatives — they show morphology neighbors ≠ same mechanism. Add ~2–4 months.

第三层：只对顶尖 1–2 个发现做活体验证Layer 3: In vivo for only 1–2 top discoveries

要验证什么What to test

细胞系里站得住的最强未知发现，在小鼠等活体模型里是否仍有表型或药效/毒性可读出。这一层贵、慢，只能挑极少几条。Whether the strongest unknown hits that held in cell lines still show phenotype or efficacy/toxicity in mice (or similar). Expensive and slow — only a few edges.

打算怎么做How to do it

从第二层筛出的 top 命中里选 1–2 个；按具体故事选合适肿瘤或药理模型（给药、敲低/敲除株、或二者联用）。读出侧重肿瘤生长、组织病理、关键通路标记——与细胞系结论对齐。Pick 1–2 tops from Layer 2; choose tumor/pharmacology models that fit the story (dosing, KD/KO lines, or both). Readouts: tumor growth, histopathology, key pathway markers — aligned with cell-line conclusions.

怎样算成功/失败Success / failure

活体方向与细胞系故事一致，或出现可解释差异。这是冲高影响力文章的收束证据，周期与预算单独单列，往往还要数月。In vivo direction matches the cell-line story, or differences are explainable. Closing evidence for high-impact papers; timeline/budget separately, often months more.

已知对照：浅做几条就够Known controls: a few shallow ones suffice

要验证什么What to test

证明邻居检索不是只会挖噪声，也能抓住文献里已经比较清楚的关系。Show neighbor retrieval is not only noise — it also recovers literature-clear relationships.

打算怎么做How to do it

例如：喜树碱与奥罗拉激酶抑制剂/氟达拉滨的细胞周期重叠；PP-2 与凋亡蛋白 caspase-3 活化；洛伐他汀加回甲羟戊酸或香叶基香叶基焦磷酸后应力纤维恢复。嵌在第一、二层里浅做即可。E.g. camptothecin vs Aurora inhibitor / fludarabine cycle overlap; PP-2 vs caspase-3 activation; lovastatin ± mevalonate/GGPP stress-fiber rescue. Nest shallowly inside Layers 1–2.
